\section{Distinguishing the Study B dense recipe}

Study B: Dense-Oriented Adaptation specifies five explicit negatives, temperature 0.05, BF16 precision, a cosine schedule, and warmup ratio 0.05. It asks for a true contrastive batch minimum of 32 and preference of 64, all-positive treatment, and B-parsed-only supervision in its initial stage. These specifications differ from Study A's seven negatives, temperature 0.02, FP16, and selected forward-pass batch four. Study B must not be used to explain how the Study A checkpoints were trained.
