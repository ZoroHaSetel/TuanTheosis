% \chapter*{Abstract}

% Vietnamese legal question answering requires retrieval of the legal provisions that support an answer. This thesis evaluates lexical–semantic hybrid retrieval using BM25's evaluation scores as the baseline. It investigates whether combining BM25 with BGE-M3 improves retrieval effectiveness, whether adding multilingual-E5-base provides further improvement, and whether fine-tuning the BGE-M3 embedding model improves both standalone and hybrid retrieval.

% The study evaluates the ALQAC and Zalo datasets using clean reference text and article text extracted from legal documents collected in raw HTML format. It also introduces a Bộ Công an (BCA) dataset built from published public-service questions and legal citations in their answers. The evaluated models are BM25, pretrained BGE-M3, pretrained multilingual-E5-base, BM25+BGE-M3, and BM25+BGE-M3+E5. NDCG@10 is the primary evaluation metric. BCA evaluation also analyzes the difficulty of retrival on real user questions and reasons for retrieval failures.

% Both hybrid configurations achieve higher NDCG@10 than BM25 across the reported ALQAC and Zalo conditions. BCA's results show the same pattern. Adding E5 to BM25+BGE-M3 increases NDCG@10 by 0.0130 on Zalo with article text extracted from HTML. The change is small or negative in the other ALQAC and Zalo conditions. For BCA's smaller groups of questions linked by shared legal references, the results do not provide sufficient evidence that adding E5 improves retrieval. BCA also shows that some target provisions are absent from the corpus, limiting retrieval effectiveness independently of the ranking method.

% Fine-tuning produces different outcomes depending on how the model is selected and evaluated. Selecting the fine-tuned BGE-M3 model based on hybrid retrieval performance improves development scores. However, evaluation on validation queries shows mean NDCG@10 decreases of 0.0520 for BM25+BGE-M3 and 0.0249 for BM25+BGE-M3+E5. A separate experiment that fine-tunes BGE-M3 for standalone dense retrieval increases mean NDCG@10 by 0.0189, but does not improve hybrid retrieval when same weights are used.
