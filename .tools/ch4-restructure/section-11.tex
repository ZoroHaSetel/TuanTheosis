\section{Chapter Summary}
\label{sec:method-summary}

This chapter presents the research design, the datasets and corpus preparation, and the construction of BCA from public questions and published answers. It specifies lexical and dense retrieval, score normalization and fusion, and BGE-M3 fine-tuning with model selection based on either hybrid or standalone dense retrieval. The evaluation design explains query grouping, data splits, metric comparisons, and the scope of statistical analysis. The chapter also documents the experimental environment, reproducibility requirements, and data-use limitations; Chapter 5 reports the results.
